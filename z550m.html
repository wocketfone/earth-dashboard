From the published description

  • Ten glow cells, with digits cut out of a silver plate.
  • Digits run clockwise, with 0 opposite 5. That's from photos of a 1962 tube.
  • Digits are about 3 mm high. They're drawn to scale here, on a 30 mm face.
  • The supply is unsmoothed and rectified, so the glow dies and re-strikes every cycle.
  • Strike needs about 105 V with a starter primed, and about 135 V without.

Assumed until we measure

  • The glow holds until the supply falls below 85 V.
  • The face is 30 mm across.
  • Two primed starters share the strikes at random.
  • A supply peak above 135 V with no starter strikes a random cell.
