# Earth Dashboard: Channel Names

Version 1.1, 13 November 2026. This is the readable copy of the naming document. The Word version sits beside it.

## Why do the tubes have these names?

The nine names make the instrument read like a piece of mid-century laboratory equipment, not a dashboard. Each is a short form built on a Greek or Latin root. They're short so they can sit beside a single-digit tube without competing with it.

## What are the nine names?

| Label | Root and meaning | System | What it measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| LITHO | Greek *lithos*, stone | The solid Earth | Earthquake energy |
| SOLAR | Latin *sol*, sun | The Sun | Sunspot number |
| CYCLO | Greek *kyklos*, circle or cycle | The Pacific Ocean cycle | El Niño and La Niña index |
| ATMOS | Greek *atmos*, vapour | The atmosphere | Surface air temperature |
| OCEAN | Greek *ōkeanos*, ocean | The oceans | Sea surface temperature |
| CRYO | Greek *kryos*, frost | The ice | Arctic and Antarctic sea ice |
| CHEMO | Combining form *chemo-*, chemical | Atmospheric chemistry | Carbon dioxide |
| ANTH | Greek *anthrōpos*, human | Human energy use | World primary energy consumption |
| PALIN | Greek *palin*, again or anew | Renewable energy | Renewable share of world electricity |

## What changed in version 1.1?

- **BIO became CYCLO.** No program-readable source exists for global plant growth. The Pacific cycle has one, and it's the liveliest honest signal on the panel.
- **TECH became PALIN.** Data-centre electricity is only published in yearly reports that a person has to type in. The renewable share can be fetched, and it gives the panel one tube where leaning right is good news. PALIN is the root of palingenesis, which means regeneration.
- **The tubes show one digit each**, not three.

## How are the tubes grouped on the panel?

Three clusters of three, so like measurements read together.

| Cluster | Tubes | What they share |
|---|---|---|
| Natural rhythms | LITHO, SOLAR, CYCLO | They come and go by themselves. None of them counts toward a reset. |
| Climate | ATMOS, OCEAN, CRYO | Air, sea, and ice: how the planet is responding. |
| Human | CHEMO, ANTH, PALIN | What we emit, what we use, and what we renew. |

The grouping is about where tubes sit on the panel. The order inside the frame file is separate, and is listed in `docs/mapping-rules.md`.

## What rules do the names follow?

- **Scientific character.** They draw on geology, oceanography, atmospheric science, chemistry, human systems, and solar physics.
- **Compact.** Short, uppercase, and balanced for a small label.
- **Timeless.** No software-era words.
- **Distinct.** Each is recognisable alone and belongs to the family.
- **Meaning before decoration.** A label points at the system. It never stands in for the definition, which lives in the rule sheet.
