Normal is the 2001–2020 value unless stated. These are the numbers the live fetcher uses.

What rules is it following?

  • Every press sweeps 0 to 9, falls back to 5, idles, then walks out one digit at a time.
  • A press during the sweep or idle changes where the tubes walk to.
  • A press during the walk-out starts the sweep again.
  • Every tube shows the freshest figure its source has.
  • When new data arrives at rest, tubes walk quietly to the new reading. There's no sweep.
  • A frame file that fails any check is rejected whole, and the last good one stays.
  • When tubes have been pinned for a year, the fetcher builds a new scale and lights the lamp by the covered button.
  • Pressing the covered button moves the board to the new scale, and the reset colour comes on. Button 4 then shows where the old normal sits.
  • A lamp by the tube's name lights when its source reports more slowly than the button's window.
  • A tube goes dark only when it has no reading at all.
  • The lamp test steps every tube through 0 to 9, at one second a digit.
  • 0 only appears in the sweep and the lamp test. It's never a reading.
